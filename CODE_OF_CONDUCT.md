# Code of conduct

Xfiles follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- be respectful and welcoming;
- give and take constructive feedback gracefully;
- focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Use the contact on the maintainers' profiles, or the repository's private reporting where it is enabled. Reports are kept confidential and handled promptly.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they find inappropriate, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
